👨‍🍳 Your Personal Specialist Chef

A dedicated Gujarati chef accompanies every Shubh Yatra group from Day 1 to the last day. He liaises with each hotel's kitchen team in advance, brings specialist ingredients, and prepares all three meals personally — ensuring every bite meets the pure vegetarian standard your family deserves. No surprises, no compromise.

🌅

Gujarati Breakfasts

Start every morning the right way — thepla, dhokla, poha, upma, fafda-jalebi, handvo, batata vada, fresh chutney and chai masala. Full spread each day in the hotel dining room.

☀️

Touring Day Thali Lunches

On coach touring days, your chef prepares freshly made thali lunches — rotli, dal, shaak, rice, kadhi, farsan, chaas and dessert. On city days, select premium pure vegetarian restaurants are used.

🌙

Regional Gujarati Dinners

Each evening a different regional menu: Surati, Kathiyawadi, Saurashtra. Signature dishes include undhiyu, dal dhokli, patra, ringan nu shaak, lasan ki chutney, ponk. Special celebration dinners at castle hotels.

🎉

Gala Castle Dinners

Special festive Gujarati gala dinners at Ashford Castle in Ireland and Gleneagles in Scotland — traditional dress encouraged. Unforgettable evenings in the grand dining rooms of two of the world's greatest hotels.

🌿

Jain Options — Full Tour

Jain meals (no potato, onion, garlic, carrot, root ginger, or underground vegetables) available for every single meal throughout the journey. Please advise at time of booking. Same menu, no additional cost.

Day 1 Arrival in London — The Royal Welcome ▼

Day 2 Iconic London — Palaces, Tower & Thames ▼
🌅 Breakfast ☀️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
Full Gujarati breakfast prepared by your chef. Morning coach tour of London — Buckingham Palace (Changing of the Guard if scheduled), Westminster Abbey, Big Ben, Houses of Parliament, Trafalgar Square, Piccadilly Circus, Tower Bridge. Tower of London entrance with Beefeater guide — Crown Jewels, White Tower, 1,000 years of royal history. Packed Gujarati thali lunch on the South Bank. Afternoon: Thames River Cruise — see London from the water, with views of the City skyline, HMS Belfast and Greenwich. Evening: Dinner at London's finest pure vegetarian restaurant (Sagar, Govinda's or equivalent).
🌿 Breakfast & lunch by your chef · Dinner: premium London veg restaurant
Day 3 Windsor Castle, Stonehenge & Kensington ▼
🌅 Breakfast ☀️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
Gujarati breakfast. Coach to Windsor Castle — the world's oldest and largest inhabited castle, official residence of the King. Tour the magnificent State Apartments, St George's Chapel (where royal weddings take place), and Queen Mary's Dolls' House. Walk through Windsor's elegant Georgian town. Packed thali lunch en route. Continue to Stonehenge on Salisbury Plain — 5,000-year-old UNESCO World Heritage Site, one of the world's greatest mysteries. Return to London. Evening: Optional visit to Harrods, Knightsbridge (extensive vegetarian and Jain food hall) or V&A Museum. Chef dinner at hotel.
🌿 All meals by your chef · Thali lunch en route
Day 4 London → Wales · Cardiff Castle & Celtic Manor ▼
🌅 Breakfast ☀️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
Breakfast and checkout. Coach journey west across the Severn Bridge into Wales (approx. 3 hours). Morning arrival in Cardiff, the Welsh capital. Visit Cardiff Castle in the city centre — a magnificent Norman fortress with extraordinarily ornate Victorian Gothic interiors (Moorish, medieval and Arabic styled rooms — reminiscent of Indian palace architecture!). Short drive to Caerphilly Castle — the largest castle in Wales, complete moated fortress dating to 1268. Thali lunch near Caerphilly. Afternoon: Check in at Celtic Manor Resort, Newport — a spectacular 5★ resort in 2,000 acres of Welsh countryside, host of the 2010 Ryder Cup. Evening: Chef dinner at Celtic Manor.
🌿 All meals by your chef
Day 5 Snowdonia National Park & Conwy Castle ▼
🌅 Breakfast ☀️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
Breakfast at Celtic Manor. Full-day excursion into North Wales. Drive through the spectacular Snowdonia National Park — Wales' most dramatic mountain landscape with peaks rising over 3,500 feet. Stop at the beautiful village of Betws-y-Coed for a gentle riverside walk. Visit the medieval walled town of Conwy and its magnificent 13th-century castle built by Edward I, with eight round towers still perfectly intact — a true fortress. Optional ride on the Snowdon Mountain Railway (weather permitting) — ascending Britain's highest mountain south of Scotland. Packed chef-prepared thali lunch. Return to Celtic Manor. Evening: Chef dinner.
🌿 All meals by your chef · Packed thali lunch en route
Day 6 Wales → Ireland · Dublin — Heritage & Georgian Beauty ▼
🌅 Breakfast ☀️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
Breakfast and checkout. Short flight from Cardiff or Bristol to Dublin (approx. 1 hour). Check in at The Shelbourne Hotel — a Dublin landmark since 1824, overlooking the beautiful St Stephen's Green in the Georgian heart of the city. Afternoon: Dublin Castle guided tour — the seat of British rule in Ireland for 700 years, now housing spectacular State Apartments. Walk through the cobblestone lanes of Temple Bar and the elegant Georgian squares. Visit Trinity College Dublin — one of the world's great universities, founded 1592. See the ancient Book of Kells — a 9th-century illuminated manuscript regarded as Ireland's greatest national treasure. Evening: Chef dinner at The Shelbourne.
🌿 All meals by your chef
Day 7 Cliffs of Moher, Galway & Ashford Castle ▼
🌅 Breakfast ☀️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
Breakfast. Epic day on Ireland's Wild Atlantic West Coast. Drive through The Burren — a vast, otherworldly limestone plateau unlike anywhere else on Earth, carpeted in rare wildflowers. Arrive at the Cliffs of Moher — 700-foot sea cliffs stretching 8km along the Atlantic coast, one of Ireland's most breathtaking natural spectacles. Walk the cliff-top path for extraordinary views. Lunch in the colourful city of Galway — famous for its medieval arches, buskers, Connaught Street markets and West of Ireland character. Pure vegetarian restaurant in Galway city. Afternoon drive north to Ashford Castle, Co. Mayo — your home for the next two nights. This 800-year-old castle on the shores of Lough Corrib was recently voted the No.1 Hotel in the World. Check-in is extraordinary. Evening: Gujarati Gala Celebration Dinner — your chef's finest spread in the castle's private dining room. Traditional dress warmly encouraged.
🌿 Breakfast & lunch by your chef · GALA GUJARATI DINNER at Ashford Castle
Day 8 Ashford Castle — Falconry, Lough Corrib & Cong Village ▼
🌅 Breakfast ☀️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
A day to savour one of the world's greatest hotels at your own pace. Ashford Castle School of Falconry — world-famous, you'll fly a hawk or eagle from your own gloved hand across the castle grounds under expert tuition. An experience never forgotten! Cruise on Lough Corrib — Ireland's largest lake, with medieval island ruins visible. Walk the castle's 350 acres of woodland, formal gardens and lakeshore. The enchanting village of Cong (where the classic film The Quiet Man was shot) is a short walk — see the ruined Cong Abbey. Afternoon at leisure — spa, tennis, cycling or simply sit by the lake with chai. All meals by your chef at the castle, prepared in the castle kitchens.
🌿 All meals by your chef at Ashford Castle
Day 9 Ireland → Scotland · Edinburgh Castle & Royal Mile ▼
🌅 Breakfast ☀️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
Breakfast at Ashford Castle. Checkout and transfer to Dublin or Shannon Airport. Flight to Edinburgh or Glasgow (approx. 1 hour). Coach to Edinburgh — Scotland's magnificent capital, a UNESCO World Heritage City. Check in at The Balmoral Hotel — Edinburgh's most iconic address, beside Waverley Station with views of the castle. Afternoon: Edinburgh Castle — perched dramatically on an extinct volcano, housing the Crown Jewels of Scotland, the Stone of Destiny and the Great Hall. Walk the famous Royal Mile — historic closes, St Giles' Cathedral, the Palace of Holyroodhouse. Evening: Chef dinner at The Balmoral.
🌿 All meals by your chef
Day 10 Edinburgh — Arthur's Seat, New Town & Leisure ▼
🌅 Breakfast ☀️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
Breakfast at hotel. Morning: hike to Arthur's Seat — the extinct volcano rising above Holyrood Park, with 360° panoramic views across Edinburgh, the Firth of Forth and beyond (45-minute climb, suitable for all fitness levels). Alternatively: Scottish National Gallery — masterpieces from Titian to Ramsay. Afternoon: Edinburgh New Town — Princes Street shopping, George Street's Georgian architecture, Scott Monument (climb 287 steps for extraordinary views). Royal Botanic Garden Edinburgh. Packed thali lunch by your chef. Evening: dinner at Edinburgh's best pure vegetarian Indian restaurant or chef dinner at hotel.
🌿 All meals by your chef or premium veg restaurant
Day 11 Loch Ness, Glencoe & The Scottish Highlands ▼
🌅 Breakfast ☀️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
Breakfast. Full-day Highland excursion. Drive north through Perthshire and across Drumochter Pass (the highest main road in Britain). Cruise on Loch Ness — Scotland's most famous loch, keeping watch for the legendary monster. Visit the dramatic ruins of Urquhart Castle on the loch shore — one of Scotland's largest castles, with extraordinary views. Return through the awe-inspiring valley of Glencoe — scene of the 1692 massacre, flanked by towering mountains and waterfalls. Packed chef thali lunch at the lochside. Stop at Rannoch Moor — one of Europe's last great wildernesses. Check in at Gleneagles Hotel, Perthshire — one of the world's truly legendary hotels, set in 850 acres of Perthshire countryside. Evening: Chef dinner at Gleneagles.
🌿 All meals by your chef · Packed thali lunch en route
Day 12 Gleneagles — Loch Lomond, Stirling Castle & Gala Dinner ▼
🌅 Breakfast ☀️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
Breakfast at Gleneagles. Morning excursion: Loch Lomond — the largest freshwater lake in Great Britain by surface area, the "bonnie banks" of Scottish song. Cruise from Balloch across its shimmering waters, surrounded by wooded hillsides. Visit Luss Village — Scotland's prettiest village, with slate-roofed cottages and a medieval church. Continue to Stirling Castle — one of Scotland's grandest castles, rivalling Edinburgh in historical importance; birthplace of Mary Queen of Scots. Packed thali lunch. Return to Gleneagles in the afternoon. Time to enjoy the hotel's famous grounds, spa and afternoon tea. Evening: Farewell Gala Gujarati Dinner at Gleneagles — your chef's most elaborate spread to mark the final evening together. Traditional dress encouraged. Toast the journey!
🌿 All meals by your chef · FAREWELL GALA GUJARATI DINNER at Gleneagles
Day 13 Departure — With Memories of a Lifetime ▼
🌅 Breakfast ☀️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
Final Gujarati breakfast at Gleneagles. Late checkout. Coach transfer to Edinburgh Airport or Glasgow Airport (approx. 45–60 minutes). Your tour manager assists with check-in and baggage. Departure flights home. Shubh Yatra ends — 13 days, 4 countries, 37 pure vegetarian meals, memories that will last a lifetime. Jai ho!
